About us

A new consultancy, built on old-fashioned attention to detail

FAB+ Plus is a management systems consultancy based in Bijnor, Uttar Pradesh, helping businesses across India reach and maintain ISO, information-security, and food-safety certification.

We're a new practice. Rather than hide that, we'd rather explain what it means for you: every project we take on right now gets direct attention from a senior consultant — not handed off to a junior team member while a partner's name sits on the proposal. That's not a permanent feature of being small; it's a deliberate choice about how we want to work as we grow.

What we believe about certification

Too many certification projects turn into a scramble in the final two weeks before an audit — documents written the night before, processes described on paper that don't match what actually happens on the floor. We think that approach produces a certificate, but not a system that actually helps your business.

Our approach is slower on purpose. We'd rather spend proper time on the gap assessment, so the documentation we help you build reflects how your business actually runs — which means it holds up under audit, and more importantly, it's still useful to you a year later when the auditor comes back for surveillance.

How we work

  • Fixed-scope pricing, agreed upfront. You'll know the full cost before we start — no hourly surprises partway through a project.
  • We attend the actual audit. We don't just prepare documents and step back. We're in the room with you when the certifying body arrives.
  • One point of contact throughout. The consultant who runs your gap assessment is the same person who sees you through certification.

Where we're based, and who we work with

We're based in Bijnor, Uttar Pradesh, and work with businesses across India — in person where it's practical, and remotely for documentation, planning, and audit preparation everywhere else. Certification requirements don't change based on your city, and neither does the attention your project gets from us.
