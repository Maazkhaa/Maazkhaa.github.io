Overview

How product inspection differs from certification

Inspection is a point-in-time, third-party check of a specific batch or shipment against buyer specifications, BIS standards, or the relevant international standard — distinct from a management-system certification that covers your whole operation on an ongoing basis.

01

Reduces buyer rejection risk

Catching non-conformities before shipment is far cheaper than a rejected container or a damaged buyer relationship.

02

Satisfies letters of credit

Many international buyers and banks require an independent pre-shipment inspection report before releasing payment.

03

Supports domestic compliance

Inspection against BIS and other applicable Indian standards helps de-risk domestic sales as well as exports.

04

Fast turnaround

Unlike certification, inspections are typically arranged and completed within days, fitting around your production and shipping schedule.

Our process

How we get you certified

Inspection follows a simpler, faster sequence than certification, since each engagement covers a specific batch or shipment rather than your whole management system.

01

Assess

We confirm the applicable standard or buyer specification and agree the inspection scope with you.

02

Implement

We schedule the inspection around your production or shipping timeline.

03

Internal audit

Our inspector examines the batch or shipment on site, sampling and testing as required.

04

Certification audit

We issue a detailed inspection report covering conformity, defects, and any corrective actions needed.

05

Maintain

We're available for repeat inspections on future shipments under the same or updated scope.
