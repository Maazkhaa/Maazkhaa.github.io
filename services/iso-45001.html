Overview

What ISO 45001 actually requires

ISO 45001:2018 sets requirements for identifying workplace hazards, assessing and controlling the risks they create, and continually improving safety performance — complementing statutory obligations under the Factories Act and India's newer OSH Code.

01

Fewer workplace incidents

Structured hazard identification and risk assessment (HIRA) catches unsafe conditions before they cause an injury.

02

Stronger tender eligibility

Principal employers and government tenders increasingly require contractor safety certification as a prequalification condition.

03

Reduced liability exposure

Documented risk controls and incident procedures strengthen your position if an incident is ever investigated.

04

Better workforce confidence

Visible safety systems tend to reduce staff turnover on sites where physical risk is a real daily concern.
