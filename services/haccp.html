Overview

What HACCP and GMP actually require

HACCP identifies the points in your process where food safety hazards are most likely to occur and puts controls in place at exactly those points. GMP covers the surrounding hygiene, facility, and staff practices that make those controls reliable.

01

Opens doors with larger buyers

Export buyers, large retail chains, and hotel groups often require HACCP from suppliers as a baseline condition, not a bonus.

02

Goes beyond your FSSAI license

An FSSAI license lets you legally operate; HACCP demonstrates an active system for preventing contamination, which is what buyers actually want to see.

03

Fewer recalls and rejections

Identifying critical control points catches problems before products leave your facility, not after a complaint.

04

Clearer staff accountability

Documented monitoring means hygiene and safety practices don't depend on any one person remembering to do them.
