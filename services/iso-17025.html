Overview

What ISO 17025 actually requires

ISO/IEC 17025:2017 sets requirements for the technical competence of testing and calibration laboratories — covering method validation, measurement uncertainty, equipment traceability, and staff competency, alongside a management system layer similar to ISO 9001.

01

Legal recognition of test reports

Accredited results carry weight with regulators, customers, and export markets that a non-accredited lab's reports don't.

02

Access to NABL accreditation

In India, ISO 17025 is the technical basis for NABL (National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories) accreditation under QCI.

03

Fewer disputed results

Formal measurement uncertainty and method validation reduce the chance of a result being challenged by a client or regulator.

04

Competitive differentiation

Accredited labs are frequently preferred or required by larger industrial and government clients.
