Overview

What ISO 27001 actually requires

ISO/IEC 27001 sets requirements for an information security management system (ISMS) — identifying risks to data you hold, selecting controls to address them, and proving those controls actually operate.

01

Supports DPDP Act obligations

A working ISMS gives you the operational backbone to meet many of the practical expectations of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, though certification and legal compliance are not the same thing.

02

Wins enterprise and government deals

Larger clients increasingly run vendor security questionnaires that a certified ISMS answers in one document instead of dozens of emails.

03

Lower breach risk

Formal risk assessment surfaces gaps — like unmanaged access or missing backups — before they become incidents.

04

Faster vendor onboarding

A current Statement of Applicability speeds up procurement reviews that would otherwise take weeks.
